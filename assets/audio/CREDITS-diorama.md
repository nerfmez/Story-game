# Sounds of the diorama scenes

Used by `scripts/konbini_summer.gd` (the beach, headland and coast on a clear day).
Both were normalised in loudness, given a short fade in and out, and re-encoded as Ogg Vorbis.

- `sea_waves.ogg`: "Sea waves" by Ganesh Mohan T, Wikimedia Commons,
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sea_waves.wav. Licence CC BY-SA 4.0
  (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). This edited version is shared under the same licence.
- `cicadas.ogg`: "蝉鸣" (cicada song) by Ngguls, Wikimedia Commons,
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:%E8%9D%89%E9%B8%A3.ogg. Licence CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication).
